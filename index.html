FAQ

Quick questions

Is it free?

Yes. If a game has a cost, you pay the organizer directly.

When can I try it?

Soon, in the US. Join the waitlist and we'll send your beta invite.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. We're starting with iPhone.

Who can join?

Anyone 16 or older. Private groups need an invite from their organizer.

What's a drop-in game?

A public game anyone nearby can join. You'll find them in Discover.
