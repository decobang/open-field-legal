FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Open Field free?

Yes, Open Field is free to use. Some games have a cost per player set by the organizer, which you pay them directly, outside the app.

When can I try it?

We're opening a beta on iPhone soon. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the invite as soon as it's ready.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Open Field is launching on iPhone first, and Android is planned for later.

Who can join?

Anyone 16 or older. Public groups and drop-in games are open to everyone nearby; private groups need an invite code from their organizer.

What's a drop-in game?

A public game that anyone nearby can join, even if they aren't in the group that runs it. Drop-ins show up in Discover.

Where is Open Field available?

In the United States for now.
